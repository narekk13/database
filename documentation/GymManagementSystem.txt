Gym Management System

1. Database Tables

	gym_customers:
		user_id (PK)
		username
		passkey
		age 
		gender 
		subscription_id
		subscription_start
		subscription_end
		trainer_id
	
	subscription:
	    sub_id 
	    sub_type 
	    price
    
    trainers:
        trainer_id
        trainer_name
        gender
        age
        price
        
2. Relationships

    A subscription can be purchased by many customers.
    
    A trainer can train many customers.
    
    Each customer belongs to one subscription.
    
    Each customer can be assigned to one trainer.
    
    A customer might not have a trainer assigned
 
3. Required Constraints
    
    Every table must have a PRIMARY KEY.
    
    The customer's username must be UNIQUE across the database.
    
    The customer's subscription ID cannot be null
    
    The trainer ID for a customer is optional and can accept null values
